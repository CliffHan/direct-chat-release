{: #responsibilities}

Because there is no server, responsibility sits with you:

- **Your device.** Keep it charged, online, and secured with a screen lock when you want your conversations protected.
- **Your keys and data.** Your device key, identity, address book, and chat history exist on your device and nowhere else. Backing them up and restoring them is your responsibility. Where the Software supports encrypted backup and import, use it — export before you reset or move to a new device, and keep both the file and its password safe, because neither can be recovered. A key that was never backed up is lost for good; an identity can be restored from a backup, or from another device that still holds it.
- **Your identity.** No server vouches for anyone. Before you trust a contact, verify that the identity you see is really theirs, using an out-of-band channel if it matters.
- **Your contacts.** The Software shows you only what peers publish; you decide whom to add and what to believe.
